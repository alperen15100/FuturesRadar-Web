Astrielle AI Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026 · A product by Ecrin Labs
Summary. Astrielle AI is an AI image-generation client. Image-generation requests require an internet connection and are processed by third-party AI infrastructure. Generated images and favorites are stored locally on your device unless you export or share them.

Google Play edition

The Google Play edition runs in Play Safe mode and blocks adult or sexualized prompts in addition to the app's baseline safety rules. The separately distributed Direct edition is intended for adults and includes an 18+ first-run confirmation.

Information processed

When you generate an image, prompt text, optional details, negative prompt, selected style, image shape and generation settings are transmitted to the configured generation service, currently a Perchance.org generator and related image-generation infrastructure. Those services may receive network and request metadata under their own policies.

Astrielle AI does not require account registration and does not request contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or approximate location permissions from Android.

Local storage

Generated images, prompts, creation timestamps and favorite status are stored in the app's private local storage. You can clear the local gallery from Settings. Exported images remain in your device's Pictures/Astrielle AI folder until you delete them.

Safety reports

The app includes an in-app safety reporting flow. A report can include a randomly generated report identifier, reason, image identifier, associated prompt, app edition, app version and timestamp. Reports are submitted over HTTPS to a form endpoint operated for Ecrin Labs and processed through Netlify Forms so the developer can review safety feedback. If delivery fails, the app can keep the report queued locally and retry later.

Third-party services

Generation currently relies on Perchance.org and related image-generation infrastructure. Safety-report delivery may use Netlify Forms. Their applicable privacy practices and terms govern their processing.

Security and retention

Network requests use HTTPS. Local generation history remains on the device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Successfully delivered safety reports are removed from the local retry queue. Third-party retention is subject to the applicable provider's practices.

Your choices

You may clear the local gallery, avoid exporting or sharing images, or stop using the app. Use the in-app report flow for a safety concern.

Contact

Privacy questions: ecrincrypto@gmail.com

Data deletion requests

Astrielle AI does not use user accounts. You can clear locally stored Gallery and Favorites data from the app. To request deletion of safety-report data or other Astrielle AI data held by Ecrin Labs, use the data deletion request form. Data processed independently by third-party generation services is subject to their own privacy practices and retention policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when material data practices, providers, SDKs or features change.
