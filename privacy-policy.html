Privacy Policy

Publisher and contact: Ecrin Ece, Türkiye — ecrincrypto@gmail.com. Updated 11 September 2026.

Data used by this website

The scanner requests public market data directly from Binance services. Those requests disclose normal connection information, such as your IP address, to the receiving service. Hosting and security providers also receive technical request information when serving pages. FuturesRadar does not ask for exchange passwords, private keys or trading API credentials.

Storage in your browser

Language, favorites, recent signal observations and cookie preferences are kept in local browser storage. These are device-specific and can be removed through your browser settings. Recent signal observations are not a record of executed trades. The calculators process the values you enter in your browser and do not place orders.

Optional services

Google Analytics is loaded after analytics consent. Google AdSense is loaded after advertising consent. These providers may use cookies and process device and usage information under their own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites. Advertising availability also depends on Google approval and account settings.

Your choices and contact

Use Cookie preferences to change future optional loading; saving a choice reloads the page. Previously created provider cookies may remain and can be removed through browser controls. External sites you choose to open have their own policies. If you email us, the address and message you provide are used to handle your inquiry. Send privacy questions to the contact above; do not include sensitive account credentials.
