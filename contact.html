Reporting a data or calculation issue

Include the page URL, symbol, approximate time with timezone, the displayed value and what you expected. A screenshot can help, but remove account balances and other private details. For a calculator issue, include the inputs needed to reproduce the result.

Editorial corrections and privacy

For a correction, identify the sentence or table and provide the source or arithmetic that supports the change. For a privacy question, describe which feature you used and what information you are asking about. Please do not send passwords, seed phrases, private keys or exchange API credentials. FuturesRadar does not need them.

This mailbox does not place or cancel exchange orders and does not provide emergency trading support. For account or execution problems, contact your exchange through its official channels.
