Funding Cost Calculator

Estimate the cost or receipt across a chosen number of funding payments.

Enter values to calculate.

How it works

Funding cost = position notional × funding rate / 100 × payment count. For shorts, the sign is reversed.

Worked example

For a 1,000 USDT long at a constant +0.01% rate across 3 payments, the estimated cost is 0.30 USDT. A short receives the same amount under these assumptions. A negative result indicates a receipt.

Assumptions

Rates, notional and settlement intervals can change. This estimate keeps rate and notional constant and assumes the position is eligible at every selected settlement. Enter a payment count, not a number of hours. Check the specific contract schedule.

Enter a rate per settlement, not per day

Use the funding percentage for the contract and settlement you want to model. Enter 0.01 for 0.01%, not 1. The payment-count field represents settlements at which your position is eligible. It is not a duration field and does not assume every contract has an eight-hour interval.

Compare payment and receipt

Hypothetical example: 2,000 USDT notional at +0.01% costs a long 0.20 USDT per payment, or 0.60 across three. With the same positive rate, the short result is −0.60, indicating a receipt. Change the rate to −0.01% and the signs reverse.

What if the funding rate changes?

Calculate each period separately and add the signed results. For a long with constant 2,000 USDT notional, rates of +0.01%, −0.02% and +0.015% produce +0.20, −0.40 and +0.30 USDT: a net cost of 0.10. Entering the first rate for all three would overestimate this particular scenario.

Why use notional rather than margin?

Funding in this model scales with position exposure. Entering 100 USDT margin for a 1,000 USDT position understates the estimate by a factor of ten. This tool does not retrieve your live position or future funding rates.
