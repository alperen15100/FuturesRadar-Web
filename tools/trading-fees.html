Crypto Futures Trading Fee Calculator

Estimate entry and exit trading fees from notional value and fee percentage. Use your exchange's actual fee tier rather than assuming one universal rate.

How futures trading fees are estimated

Entry fee = entry notional × entry fee rate. Exit fee = exit notional × exit fee rate. Total modeled fee = entry fee + exit fee.

Worked example

At 1,000 USDT entry notional and 0.05%, modeled entry fee is 0.50 USDT. If exit notional is 1,100 USDT at the same rate, modeled exit fee is 0.55 USDT, for 1.05 USDT total.

Why entry and exit rates are separate

Maker/taker status, fee tiers, promotions and exchange rules can differ between fills. This calculator therefore accepts separate rates. It does not retrieve live exchange fees.

What is excluded?

Funding, spread, slippage, liquidation charges, taxes and other exchange-specific costs are excluded. Continue with the P&L calculator, break-even calculator and funding calculator.
