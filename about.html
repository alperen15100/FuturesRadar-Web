About FuturesRadar

Updated 11 September 2026 · FuturesRadar

FuturesRadar is a crypto futures screening and education website published by Ecrin Ece in Türkiye. It brings a volume-selected watchlist, RSI readings, overbought candle counts and a transparent screening score into one interface.

Use the site in three steps

  1. Use the scanner to identify a contract for further investigation and check data freshness.
  2. Read the methodology to understand the coverage limits and how each score component is calculated.
  3. Use the Academy and calculators to explore hypothetical costs and position sizes with your own inputs.

What the product does not do

FuturesRadar does not execute orders, hold customer funds or connect to a personal exchange account. It is not an exchange, broker or investment adviser. A high score is not a promise of profit. Public exchange data can be delayed or unavailable, and some listed contracts use default RSI and OB values.

Accountability
