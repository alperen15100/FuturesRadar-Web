Frequently Asked Questions

Useful answers about FuturesRadar, RSI, signal interpretation and risk management.

What is FuturesRadar?

FuturesRadar is an educational crypto futures market intelligence website with a live scanner and Academy guides.

Where are the live signals?

The live scanner, Top Signals table, heatmap and signal intelligence panel are on the homepage.

How should I use a signal after it appears?

Treat it as a market alert. Open the chart, check trend, support, resistance, volume, RSI, overbought count and risk-reward before making any decision.

Should I enter immediately after a signal appears?

Not necessarily. A signal shows activity, but professional analysis requires confirmation and risk planning.

Is FuturesRadar financial advice?

No. It provides educational analytics only and does not recommend buying, selling, longing or shorting any asset.

What is RSI?

RSI is a momentum indicator used to evaluate recent price strength and weakness.

What is 24h overbought count?

It counts repeated overbought RSI conditions in a recent 24-hour window.

Does overbought mean sell?

No. Strong trends can stay overbought for a long time.

What should I check before acting?

Trend direction, support-resistance, volume, invalidation level, position size and leverage.

Why is risk management important?

Risk management protects capital and prevents one trade from causing serious damage.

Can the scanner be wrong?

Yes. Every indicator and scanner can produce misleading readings.

Does the site guarantee profit?

No. No scanner, indicator or strategy can guarantee profit.
