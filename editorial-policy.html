Editorial and corrections policy

Updated 11 September 2026 · FuturesRadar

FuturesRadar publishes educational explanations of its scanner and calculation tools. The publisher is Ecrin Ece, based in Türkiye. Our purpose is to make inputs and limitations understandable so readers can check the arithmetic themselves.

What counts as an example?

Worked scenarios use hypothetical numbers unless explicitly attributed to a dated source. They do not represent executed trades, customer results or evidence of profitability. We separate descriptions of our software from general market concepts. A scanner label is never treated as a verified outcome.

Sources and preparation

Technical descriptions are checked against the implementation and linked primary documentation where available. This content update was prepared with AI assistance and checked for calculation and implementation consistency. It has not been independently reviewed by a licensed financial adviser. We do not claim such a review or professional credentials.

Updates and corrections

An update date indicates an edit to the page, not continuous real-time verification of every statement. If a number, formula or explanation appears wrong, email ecrincrypto@gmail.com with the page URL, the disputed passage and your calculation or source. Do not send account passwords or API secrets. Corrections can change educational text without changing the scanner; changes to scanner methodology should be described on the methodology page.

Commercial context

The site uses Google AdSense integration and may display advertising when enabled and approved. An advertisement does not establish our endorsement of its claims. Paid placements or affiliate links, if introduced, should be identified where they appear. No advertiser is an input to the current scoring formula.
