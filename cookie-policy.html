Cookie Policy

Updated 11 September 2026. FuturesRadar stores language, favorites, local signal history and your cookie choice in browser local storage. These settings support the features you use and are distinct from optional advertising and analytics.

Optional loading

Google Analytics and Google AdSense scripts are not loaded by this site until the matching preference is accepted. You can reject both, enable analytics only, or accept both. The market scanner and calculators do not require either optional service.

Change a choice

Open Cookie preferences in the page footer. Saving a new choice reloads the page so future loading follows it. Changing a choice does not automatically erase cookies previously created by external providers; use browser settings to remove them. Blocking browser storage may prevent preferences and favorites from persisting.
