How To Analyze FuturesRadar Signals

FuturesRadar signals are market intelligence alerts, not automatic trade instructions. When a coin appears in the Top Signals table, use it as a starting point for analysis. A professional decision should combine momentum, trend direction, volume, support-resistance and risk management.

1. Check Signal QualityStart with Score, RSI 15m and 24h OB Count. A high score shows activity, not certainty.
2. Open the ChartClick the coin and inspect trend direction, recent highs, recent lows, breakout structure and volatility.
3. Mark Key LevelsIdentify support, resistance, invalidation level and areas where price may reject.
4. Confirm MomentumLook for higher highs, higher lows, increasing volume and controlled pullbacks. Avoid chasing vertical candles.
5. Define Risk FirstDecide maximum loss, position size, leverage and exit plan before entering any trade.
6. Wait If NeededIf structure, volume or risk-reward is unclear, waiting is better than forcing a position.

Educational note: This is not financial advice. FuturesRadar signals are informational alerts. Crypto futures involve high risk.
