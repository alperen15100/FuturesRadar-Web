Risk Management

Position Sizing Guide

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Learn how position sizing protects trading capital and reduces emotional decisions.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Risk budget comes first

Position size connects an account-level loss budget to a price level where an idea would be invalidated. For a linear USDT contract, quantity multiplied by the distance between entry and stop gives the price-only loss at that stop. Margin and risk budget are different numbers.

A worked position

An account of 1,000 USDT with a chosen 1% risk budget allows 10 USDT of price-only loss. With entry at 100 and stop at 95, each unit risks 5 USDT. Quantity is 10 / 5 = 2 units, with a 200 USDT entry notional.

Where estimates fall short

Fees, funding, slippage and a gap through the stop can increase the actual loss. Reduce quantity if these costs must fit within the same budget. Check exchange quantity steps before placing an order. A calculated quantity may need to be rounded down.
