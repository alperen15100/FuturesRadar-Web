What Is Leverage in Crypto Futures?

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Learn how leverage works and why it can quickly increase both gains and losses.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Leverage and exposure

Leverage expresses position exposure relative to allocated margin. It lets a smaller margin support a larger position, but does not create an advantage in predicting direction. For a fixed position quantity, changing leverage does not change the price-based profit or loss.

Compare two positions

A 1,000 USDT position supported by 100 USDT initial margin has a simple exposure ratio of 10:1. A 1% adverse price move is about 10 USDT before costs, or 10% of that initial margin. This example excludes maintenance margin and liquidation rules.

Margin is not a stop loss

Liquidation depends on the contract, maintenance tiers, collateral and margin mode. Do not estimate liquidation by simply subtracting one divided by leverage from entry. Use position size to define risk, then check the exchange-specific margin requirements.
