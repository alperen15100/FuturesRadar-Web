RSI Below 30 Explained: Oversold Conditions in Crypto Markets

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Learn how to interpret oversold RSI readings without blindly buying falling markets.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Weak momentum can persist

RSI below 30 is commonly called oversold. The reading describes the recent balance of gains and losses; it does not show that an asset is cheap or must rebound. During a sustained decline, repeated low readings can accompany further losses.

A falling market

A coin can fall from 100 to 90 with RSI at 28, briefly recover and then fall to 80 while RSI remains low. Buying only because a threshold was crossed provides no explicit exit rule or evidence that selling pressure has ended.

What adds context

Look for price structure, the location of prior support, volatility and the behavior of completed candles. Distinguish a bounce from a trend change. The scanner OB count measures readings at or above 70, so it should not be interpreted as an oversold count.
