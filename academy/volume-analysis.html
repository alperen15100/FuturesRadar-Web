Volume Analysis Explained

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Learn how volume can confirm or weaken a crypto futures signal.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Activity over a stated interval

Volume measures traded activity, not guaranteed buying pressure. A trade has a buyer and seller. Base-asset volume and quote volume are different units; this scanner displays quote volume in USDT for its 24-hour comparisons.

An apples-to-apples comparison

A completed 15-minute candle trading 3 million USDT versus a typical 1 million USDT shows three times the chosen baseline. A candle only five minutes old is not directly comparable to a completed 15-minute candle. Define whether your baseline is a mean or median.

Combine volume and structure

A breakout with increased volume shows participation, but can still fail. Watch whether price holds beyond the level or returns into the prior range. Large 24-hour volume can reflect activity many hours ago, so it is not proof of a current burst.

Compare turnover with liquidity

Hypothetical worked example. Two contracts can each show 10 million USDT in daily quote volume while offering very different order-book depth at the moment you trade. Past turnover does not tell you the average fill price for your order.

How much does volume affect the score?

A hypothetical 1 million USDT quote volume contributes about 21.6 points; 10 million contributes about 25.2. The logarithm compresses large differences and the contribution is capped at 30.
