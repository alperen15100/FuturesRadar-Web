Trend Analysis for Beginners

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Understand uptrends, downtrends, ranging markets and trend confirmation.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Direction over a chosen horizon

A trend is a pattern of directional movement across a defined interval. An asset may rise on a daily chart while falling on a 15-minute chart. Without stating the horizon, the phrase trending upward is incomplete.

Momentum and trend differ

A strong 24-hour percentage change can result from one early jump followed by hours of sideways trading. RSI may then decline while price remains near its highs. Neither observation alone proves a reversal.

Build a repeatable reading

Choose your interval, define swing or moving-average criteria and apply them consistently. Use scanner rankings to locate active markets, then inspect the actual chart. Do not treat high score, high RSI and trend confirmation as interchangeable concepts.
