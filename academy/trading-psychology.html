Trading Psychology Guide

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Understand FOMO, fear, greed and revenge trading in crypto futures.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Reduce decisions made under pressure

A written process can help distinguish a planned action from a reaction to a fast-moving price. Common problems include chasing a move, increasing size to recover a loss and selectively remembering successful signals. A checklist does not remove uncertainty.

A useful journal entry

Record the time, symbol, setup condition, planned invalidation, position size and actual outcome. Add whether the rules were followed. A profitable trade can still violate the process, while a planned loss can be consistent with it.

Review without rewriting history

Evaluate a meaningful set of comparable observations and include costs. Avoid changing rules after each isolated result. If frustration leads to unplanned trades, step away and review later. A scanner should support a process rather than create a need to act on every update.
