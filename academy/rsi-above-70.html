RSI Above 70 Explained: Overbought Does Not Always Mean Sell

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Understand why RSI above 70 can show strong momentum, not only reversal risk.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

High momentum is not an automatic sell

RSI at or above 70 is commonly described as overbought. It means recent positive price changes have been strong relative to negative ones. Trending markets can hold elevated readings through many candles; the threshold is not a timer for reversal.

Persistence versus separate events

If RSI remains above 70 for ten consecutive 15-minute readings, the scanner can count ten overbought candles. That is one sustained episode in ordinary language, not necessarily ten independent signals or ten trading opportunities.

Questions to ask

Is price extending away from support or consolidating above a breakout? Is participation increasing? Where would your interpretation fail? Use the count as a measure of persistence and keep it separate from win rate or expected return.
