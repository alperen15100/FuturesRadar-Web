Risk Management

Stop Loss Framework

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Learn how stop losses, invalidation and risk planning work together.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Define an invalidation level

A stop order is intended to reduce a position when a trigger condition is met. It is useful to distinguish the trigger price from the eventual fill price. Fast moves and thin liquidity can produce execution away from the trigger.

Stop-market versus stop-limit

A stop-market prioritizes execution after triggering, with price uncertainty. A stop-limit sets a limit price but may remain unfilled when the market moves beyond it. Available order types and trigger-price choices depend on the exchange.

Connect stop distance to quantity

If entry is 100 and the invalidation level is 95, price risk is 5 per unit. A 10 USDT price-only budget supports 2 units. Include costs and slippage in the broader plan. Moving the stop farther away without reducing quantity increases risk.
