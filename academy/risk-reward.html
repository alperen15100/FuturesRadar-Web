Risk Management

Risk Reward Ratio Guide

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Understand risk-reward, win rate, expectancy and trade filtering.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Compare distances consistently

A reward-to-risk ratio compares potential gain at a target with potential loss at an invalidation level. For a simple linear long, reward distance is target minus entry and risk distance is entry minus stop. A favorable ratio does not make the target likely.

A 2:1 example

Entry at 100, stop at 95 and target at 110 give reward distance 10 and risk distance 5, or 2:1. Before costs, a strategy with fixed 2R wins and 1R losses needs a win rate above one third for positive average expectancy.

Include real outcomes

Fees, slippage, partial exits and missed fills change realized results. Track average wins, average losses and all qualifying observations instead of only the best examples. A theoretical target is not a realized profit.
