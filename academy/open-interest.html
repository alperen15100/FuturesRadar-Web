Open Interest Explained

FuturesRadar Academy • Practical market guide • Not financial advice

Understand open interest, market participation and how it differs from trading volume.

This guide is educational only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, long or short any asset. Crypto futures involve high risk.

Outstanding contracts

Open interest measures contracts that remain open. It is different from volume, which measures trading during a period. Every contract has two sides, so growing open interest alone cannot prove that buyers or sellers will win. Check whether a source reports contracts, asset units or notional value.

Price and participation

If price rises while open interest rises, the market has more outstanding exposure during the move. This can be consistent with new participation, but does not reveal whether new longs or hedged positions dominate. Rising price with falling open interest can reflect position closure.

Use changes with context

Compare the same contract and measurement unit over a defined interval. Notional open interest can rise just because price rises. Combine it with price structure and actual volume; do not treat an isolated percentage change as a directional signal.
