Risk Disclaimer

Updated 22 September 2026 · FuturesRadar

FuturesRadar provides educational market analytics, explanations and calculators. Nothing on this website is personalized financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and the site does not place trades or manage user funds.

Crypto futures can produce rapid losses

Futures and perpetual contracts can magnify both gains and losses. Leverage reduces the margin required for a given exposure but does not make the underlying price risk smaller. Liquidation, slippage, fees, funding and market gaps can cause outcomes to differ from a simple calculation.

Scanner labels are not trade instructions

WATCH, A, A+ and ELITE are screening categories generated from the documented scoring inputs. They are not buy, sell, long or short recommendations, forecasts, probabilities or verified performance grades. RSI and overbought counts describe historical price behavior within the scanner's calculation window.

Data can be incomplete or stale

Market data comes from public third-party services and can be delayed, unavailable or affected by network conditions. FuturesRadar has documented coverage limits, including fallback values for contracts where candle data is not fetched or fails. Always inspect freshness and read the methodology.

Calculator results are estimates

Calculators use the inputs and assumptions displayed on each tool. They do not know your exchange account, maintenance-margin tier, collateral, fee tier or actual execution price unless you manually model relevant values. A calculated stop, quantity, break-even price or cost is not a guarantee of execution or maximum loss.

Independent verification

Verify contract specifications and account-specific information with the relevant exchange before making a financial decision. Historical examples and hypothetical calculations do not guarantee future results. See the editorial policy for sourcing and correction practices, or contact us to report an error.
